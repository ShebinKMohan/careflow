# Finding your way

<!-- section: directions.ground_floor -->
## Ground floor

On the ground floor are the main entrance hall with the check-in kiosks, reception and the payment desk, the emergency department (through the doors to the left of reception), the pharmacy (to the right of the main entrance), the cafe and the prayer rooms.

<!-- section: directions.first_floor -->
## First floor

On the first floor are the general clinic with Room 1 to Room 4, its waiting area with the screen that shows the numbers called, and the family waiting corner next to Room 1.

<!-- section: directions.lifts -->
## Lifts and stairs

Two lifts are opposite reception; the stairs are next to them. A third lift, for patients on stretchers, is inside the emergency department.

<!-- section: directions.rooms -->
## Finding your room

When your number is called, the screen shows your room. Rooms 1 to 4 are on the first floor: turn left out of the lifts. Resus 1 and 2 and ED 1 to ED 3 are inside the emergency department on the ground floor; a nurse will take you there.
