# Facilities

<!-- section: facilities.prayer_room -->
## Prayer rooms

There are two prayer rooms on the ground floor, one for men and one for women, at the end of the corridor past the cafe. Washing facilities are next to them.

<!-- section: facilities.cafe -->
## Cafe

The cafe is on the ground floor, next to the main entrance hall. It is open from 7:00 to 22:00, and until 18:00 on public holidays. It sells drinks, sandwiches and snacks.

<!-- section: facilities.wifi -->
## Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is available everywhere in the clinic. Join the network "CareFlow-Guest"; no password is needed. Please do not use it to make video calls in the waiting areas.

<!-- section: facilities.toilets -->
## Toilets

There are toilets on both floors, next to the lifts. Accessible toilets and a baby-changing room are on the ground floor, next to the pharmacy.
