# Parking and arrival

<!-- section: parking.car_park -->
## Car park

The clinic car park is in front of the main entrance, with 120 spaces. The first 2 hours are free; after that parking costs a small hourly fee, paid at the machine by the exit.

<!-- section: parking.drop_off -->
## Drop-off area

You can stop for up to 10 minutes in the drop-off area at the main entrance to help a patient get out of the car. Please do not park there.

<!-- section: parking.accessible -->
## Accessible parking

There are 10 accessible parking spaces closest to the main entrance, marked with the wheelchair sign. Wheelchairs are available free of charge just inside the main entrance.
