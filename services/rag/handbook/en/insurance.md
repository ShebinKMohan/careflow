# Insurance and payment

<!-- section: insurance.accepted -->
## Insurance we accept

The clinic works directly with three insurers: Insurer A, Insurer B and Insurer C. If your plan is with one of them, show your insurance card at reception after you check in, and the clinic claims from the insurer for you. Some plans ask you to pay a small share of the cost yourself; reception will tell you the amount before you pay.

<!-- section: insurance.no_insurance -->
## Without insurance

You will always be seen in an emergency, with or without insurance. For other visits without insurance, reception gives you the price list before your visit, and you pay at the payment desk next to reception by card or cash.

<!-- section: insurance.documents -->
## What to bring

Bring your identity card or passport, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of the medicines you take. You can still check in at the kiosk without them.
