# Visitors

<!-- section: visitors.hours -->
## Visiting hours

Visitors are welcome from 10:00 to 21:00 every day. In the emergency department one companion can stay with each patient at any time.

<!-- section: visitors.rules -->
## Visitor rules

At most two visitors can be with a patient at once. Please keep your voice low in the waiting areas, keep your phone on silent, and do not bring food into the treatment rooms. Visitors who feel unwell should not visit.

<!-- section: visitors.children -->
## Children as visitors

Children under 12 may visit when an adult who is not the patient stays with them.
