# Departments

<!-- section: departments.emergency -->
## Emergency department

The emergency department sees the most urgent patients first, whatever their arrival time. It is on the ground floor, through the doors to the left of reception. Its treatment areas are Resus 1 and 2 and the rooms ED 1, ED 2 and ED 3. Queue numbers for the emergency department start with the letter A.

<!-- section: departments.general -->
## General clinic

The general clinic sees patients who need care today but not urgently. It is on the first floor. Its rooms are Room 1 to Room 4. Queue numbers for the general clinic start with the letter G, and the screen in the first-floor waiting area shows the next numbers called.

<!-- section: departments.children -->
## Children

Children are seen in the same departments as adults, and a parent or carer must stay with a child at all times. There is a quieter waiting corner for families with children on the first floor, next to Room 1, with books and toys.
