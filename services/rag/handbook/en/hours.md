# Opening hours

<!-- section: hours.opening -->
## When the clinic is open

The CareFlow Demo Clinic is open every day from 7:00 to 23:00, including weekends. Check in at the kiosk in the main entrance hall; reception is staffed during the same hours. Appointments for the general clinic start at 8:00; the last appointment is at 22:00.

<!-- section: hours.emergency -->
## The emergency department

The emergency department never closes: it is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Between 23:00 and 7:00 use the emergency entrance on the east side of the building, next to the ambulance bay; the main entrance is locked at night.

<!-- section: hours.holidays -->
## Public holidays

On public holidays the general clinic keeps its normal hours, from 7:00 to 23:00, but some services close early: the pharmacy closes at 20:00 and the cafe at 18:00. The emergency department is open as usual.
