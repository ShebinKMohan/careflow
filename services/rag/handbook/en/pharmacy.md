# Pharmacy

<!-- section: pharmacy.hours -->
## Pharmacy hours and place

The pharmacy is on the ground floor, to the right of the main entrance. It is open from 8:00 to 22:00 every day, and until 20:00 on public holidays.

<!-- section: pharmacy.prescriptions -->
## Collecting a prescription

If the doctor gives you a prescription, it is sent to the clinic pharmacy while you wait. Take your queue number to the pharmacy counter to collect it. You may also take the prescription to any other pharmacy.
