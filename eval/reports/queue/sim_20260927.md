# Queue simulation, 2026-09-27 (M1-10)

Research prototype. Synthetic data only. Not for clinical use.

`uv run python -m careflow_api.queue.sim` runs `QueueCore` itself in a seeded `heapq`
event loop. The acceptance checks run in `services/api/tests/sim/test_queue_sim.py`.

## Setup

- 8 hours of Poisson arrivals at 200/hour, seed 42.
- Level mix: 1: 2%, 2: 13%, 3: 40%, 4: 35%, 5: 10%.
- Exponential service, mean minutes by level: 1: 30, 2: 25, 3: 20, 4: 12, 5: 8.
- 66 rooms; the first 2 take only levels 1-2 (resus).
- 5% of calls are no-shows. The grace window is 120 s; a first miss re-queues the token once, a second miss makes it a no-show.
- Patients who arrived within the horizon are followed until they are seen.

## Load and fairness

- Room utilisation (service time within the horizon): 0.814.
- Calls: 1661; no-show calls: 75; tokens re-queued: 72; no-shows: 3.
- Waiting count: mean 0.79 over the run, 0.00 in the last hour; maximum 21.
- Level 1-2 tokens left waiting while a room that takes them was free: 0.
- Levels 4-5 wait longest under strict priority (their p95 is above). Aging, which would bound it, is a later decision; it is reported here, not hidden.
