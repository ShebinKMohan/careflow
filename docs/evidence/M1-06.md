**The prompt took three attempts.** The failures were real model behaviour, not test problems.

1. **Rules only.** Two expected facts were missing. The 9B model left `infection.fever_reported` out of "I've had a high fever and chills". It also returned the sentence "Yes, I fell on it." as the value of `injury`, which the verifier correctly dropped as not a yes/no value.
2. **More explicit rules** ("extract everything the patient states", "yes/no values are true or false"). Same result, and the first request timed out at 20 s on a cold server.
3. **One worked example** on a different scenario (a cough with fever, a hand hurt by a car door), plus the warm-up. All 3 transcripts met every expectation.

The model's accuracy on a larger set is M1-18's eval suite. No accuracy figure is claimed here.

## Deviations from the issue text

- **Vital signs are not extracted from the patient's words.** Devices and nurses provide them. Coded, EHR and model-inferred keys are not extracted either: 45 keys are allowed. A fact with any other key is dropped.
- **Retry-error truncation.** The retry once with the error text is the provider layer's existing `structured()`. It now cuts the errors to 2,000 characters for every task, instead of the extractor re-implementing a retry.
- **"Unit" is ignored.** No extractable key has a unit: vital signs are excluded, and age and pain are whole numbers.
- **Contradictory answers are dropped.** A yes/no value that is true but also negated is dropped, so the question can be asked again. Guessing "no" could suppress an alert.
- **`RecordedProvider`** is `ReplayProvider` itself: recorded cassettes, or scripted replies recorded as they are served. It is not a separate class.
- **The warm-up request** before timed live calls is not in the issue. It absorbs the server's first-request cost, and it is reported.
