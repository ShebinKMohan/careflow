# M2-07: the waiting room and "I feel worse", verified

Status: 27 September 2026, SHE-302. The core (ADR-038) was merged earlier; this change adds its
verification on the real stack and fixes what the review and the journeys found (ADR-049).
Research prototype; synthetic personas only; not for clinical use.

## What changed

- **The nurse's session card** shows every alert, before and after the decision, and marks one
  raised after it "New since the decision". It reloads live: on a staff-channel frame about the
  session, on every (re)connection (the snapshot), and every 5 s while the channel is paused. A
  failed reload keeps the last card. It shows the patient's "I feel worse" presses.
- **The worklist** shows a confirmed patient's banner again for an alert raised after the
  decision, and sorts that patient first again.
- **The triage view** keeps done tasks (`tasks`), so a card read again after "ECG done" does not
  show the ECG as due. It no longer fails after a decision without a model suggestion. It carries
  the presses (`feels_worse`).
- **The phone** always shows "I feel worse": a failed status poll keeps the last status, marked
  stale, and every socket (re)open reloads the status.
- **The API** refuses a press on an ended visit (409, "tell the reception desk now"), and writes
  the press's EHR Flag whether or not the rules re-run succeeds.
- **Queue controls** on the nurse worklist: "Call next" per department; "Arrived", "No-show" and
  "Seen (free the room)" for a called patient.
- **The sticky alert region** takes at most 45% of the screen (the journey found it covering the
  queue controls).
- **The M1-15 countdown flake** is fixed (docs/FLAKY.md).

(`test_intake_flow.py::test_keypad_to_token_on_the_real_stack`) now also reads the card after
"ECG done". Before this change, that read failed with a 500 (the decision without a suggestion)
and would have listed the ECG as open.

## The journey (Playwright, real stack)

`e2e/journeys/feels-worse.spec.ts`:
- **Setup.** A routine patient (100005) waits ahead in the general queue. A second patient (100006)
  hands over to a phone (Pixel 7 profile) and finishes there. The nurse sets KTAS 5 for both,
  through the card.
- **The press.** With the nurse's worklist and the patient's card both open, the patient presses
  "I feel worse" with FAST signs:
  - the banner is on the worklist within 2 s (asserted on this machine, reported on a CI runner:
    docs/FLAKY.md);
  - the FAST alert shows again, marked new, on the worklist and on the already open card, with no
    reload;
  - a second press raises the count to 2 on the same banner, and on the card.
- **The queue.** The nurse's "Call next: General" moves the phone's place up with no reload.
- **The dropped socket.** The phone's socket is cut (routed through the test) and the nurse calls
  again at once. The phone reconnects with a fresh ticket and shows the server's current status.
- **Clean-up.** The nurse frees every room the journey filled with "Seen (free the room)".

Three consecutive runs of all seven journeys (M1-19's three, M2-06's three and this one), replayed
models, stack `careflow-e2e` (+30000) from a worktree of this branch:

| Run (UTC) | Result | Total | Press → nurse's worklist | Chest-pain alert after the answer | Kiosk cleared after the claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14:29:35 | 7 passed, 0 flaky | 53.4 s | 1,161 ms | 693 ms | 5,375 ms |
| 14:30:28 | 7 passed, 0 flaky | 51.9 s | 1,175 ms | 1,188 ms | 5,378 ms |
| 14:31:21 | 7 passed, 0 flaky | 54.3 s | 1,177 ms | 1,203 ms | 5,433 ms |

The first passing run of the journey, on a stack with fewer open visits, measured 666 ms. The
worklist reads each open session one by one, so the time grows with the open visits left on a
test stack. It stays under the 2 s criterion; M6-01 owns the speed.

Handoff items:
- **The nurse view.** Its priority bug is fixed and tested: alerts after the decision, live
  refresh, the reconnect snapshot, and the poll while paused.
- **Two browser contexts.** They prove that repeated reports after the decision reach the nurse.
- **All-no, FAST, unsure child and the injected rules failure.** Covered by the pytest tests.

- **The phone's reconnect snapshot, poll fallback and duplicate frames.** Covered by the unit tests

- **Renewal.** A long wait keeps the phone's token alive (SHE-300's renewal; the waiting room uses
  the latest token).
- **Privacy and access.** Events stay free of patient text; the phone, session and clinic are
  checked.
