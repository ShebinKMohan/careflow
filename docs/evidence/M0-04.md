## Deviations from the issue text

- Extra outputs mapped beyond the list: `rules.news2_total`, `session.appointment`, `complaint_category` (Encounter.reasonCode), `alert.notification`, `write_batch.provenance` / `meta_tag`. Paths that are not facts use an `<output>.<name>` form.
- Intake answers go into one QuestionnaireResponse per answering source (patient or parent, and nurse) so each carries a single provenance tag. Nurse-answered Questionnaire items carry a CareFlow `audience` extension.
- EHR resources copied into the briefing's document Bundle are tagged `EHR`.
- The Questionnaire also includes `patient.*` items (age for walk-ins without an EHR match) and gates the NG143 amber group on age under 5. The title carries an Arabic `translation` extension as the placeholder pattern for M2-03 (machine-written).
- ADR-011 records these conventions.
- Also in this PR: the post-merge oasdiff run against `origin/main` appended to `docs/evidence/M0-03.md`.
