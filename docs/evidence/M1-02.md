## 2. Every red and every amber feature has its own scenario; all benign scenarios raise none

`test_every_red_feature_has_its_own_scenario` and `test_every_amber_feature_has_its_own_scenario` require a scenario whose `covers` names the feature and whose `expect_alerts` has the right flag. `test_single_feature_scenarios_raise_only_their_flag` requires those scenarios to raise nothing else.

- Red (15): the 13 `child_danger.*` answers (pale/mottled/ashen/blue, no response to social cues, appears ill to a healthcare professional, hard to wake, weak/high-pitched cry, grunting, chest indrawing, reduced skin turgor, non-blanching rash, bulging fontanelle, neck stiffness, seizure, focal neurological signs), RR 64 at 3 months, and 38.0 °C at 2 months.
- Amber (18 features, 21 files): the 13 `child_amber.*` answers (including the new `not_responding_normally`), rigors, RR 54 at 9 months and 44 at 30 months, SpO2 95% in air, heart rate 165 at 8 months, 155 at 18 months and 145 at 3 years, and 39.0 °C at 4 months.
- Benign (11): ankle sprain, afebrile sore throat, a 3-year-old at 38.2 °C with no other features, an adult fever with NEWS2 1, FAST all no, chest pain no, a child with normal vitals, an infant exactly at the limits (heart rate 160, RR 50), a 4-month-old at 38.8 °C, a parent answering no to every screen question, and a gradual headache. All raise no alert. The absence of an alert never means "nothing is wrong".

## 3. Chest pain: `deadline_at` equals `arrived_at` + 10 min

`chest-pain-ecg-deadline`: arrived 09:00Z, deadline 09:10:00Z. Property `test_chest_pain_deadline_is_arrival_plus_10_minutes` (500 examples): for any arrival time in 2026 and a chest-pain answer 0-600 minutes later, `deadline_at - arrived_at == 10 min`. Without an arrival time, the deadline counts from the chest-pain fact (`chest-pain-no-arrival-time`: fact 09:01, deadline 09:11).

## 4. FAST with unknown onset returns needed fact `fast.last_known_well`

`fast-onset-unknown` expects `fast.last_known_well` among the needed facts. `test_an_onset_recorded_as_unknown_is_asked_for` shows that an onset recorded as "unknown" is also asked for. With a known onset, the onset fact is in `evidence_fact_ids` and nothing is asked (`test_fast_alert_includes_the_onset_as_evidence`).

## Deviations from the issue text

- **oasdiff ignore file:** reviewed, intentional contract changes are listed in `packages/contracts/oasdiff-err-ignore.txt`, and the breaking-change check still fails on anything else.

- **New contract fact** `child_amber.not_responding_normally`. The amber sign had no M0-02 path, and the red question's old wording ("no smile") described that amber sign. The red question was reworded to match NICE.
- **Age from whole years:** each age-dependent threshold uses the end of the possible 12 months that alerts sooner (ADR-017).
- **A parent-reported seizure is red**, and **SpO2 of 95% or less on oxygen is also amber** (conservative; ADR-017).
- **Red and amber can fire together**, as the no-removal property requires.
- `fast.last_known_well` recorded as "unknown"/"unsure" counts as unknown.
