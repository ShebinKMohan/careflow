# M2-04: the child triage flow, verified (SHE-293)

Status: 27 September 2026. ADR-035 built the flow; this is its verification, and the fixes the
verification needed (ADR-052). Research prototype; synthetic personas only; not for clinical use.

## What changed

- **Explicit answers without a model.** A typed or spoken yes / no / not sure to a yes/no question
  is read before the extractor, asymmetrically: unsure first; yes when the answer begins with it;
  no only when the answer is nothing else (`careflow_intake/tap.py`, ADR-052).
  - "No, but she is very hard to wake" is never read as no.
  - An answer with more words still goes to the extractor, for the other facts it may hold.
- **An unread red question becomes unknown, never no.** When a child's red question gets no
  readable answer after its last ask (the extractor failed or found nothing), it is recorded as
  `unknown`, citing the parent's answer. The rules then alert with `parent_unsure`, and
  `extraction_incomplete` tells the nurse to check.
- **No model suggestion for a child.** A child gets none (`suggest_urgency`), at any age under 16.
  The model's own guard let a 15-year-old child through.
- **Arabic alert lines checked.** `alert_templates.yaml` (the nurse's alert lines and qualifiers)
  is now in the Arabic back-translation gate.
- **Scenarios generated.** `scripts/child_scenarios.py` writes the 101 child scenarios into
  `eval/scenarios/rules/child/`, and `make lint` checks them. They have a folder of their own
  because the eval harness replays a recorded baseline of the 56 files above it (SHE-314); the
  rules tests run both folders.

## The parent path on the real stack (`child-parent-path.spec.ts`)

Persona `child-drowsy` (keypad 100003, synthetic, 2 years old), in English and in Arabic. The steps:
- the parent picks "My child", then 2 years;
- types the complaint (the model's reply is replayed from the cassette);
- taps "Yes" to hard to wake and "No" to the 15 other questions.

The journey checks:
- **The alert.** The critical `NG143_RED_FLAG` chip reaches the nurse's already open worklist
  within 2 s of the kiosk getting the "Yes". This is a speed budget, asserted on the reference
  machine and reported on CI.
- **The fact.** It is PATIENT_STATED, and its quote is the tapped label.
- **No vitals step.** Every child question is a tap, and the kiosk never navigates to `/vitals`.
  It ends on the child's done screen: "A nurse has been told. Please stay here." and "A nurse will
  check your child's temperature and breathing."
- **No suggestion.** The triage view has none; the nurse's card says "No model suggestion".
- **The nurse still decides.** There is no token until the nurse sets the level. The kiosk then
  shows it, and the visit is closed.

Measured on the reference machine, on the e2e stack with 352 open visits, replaying the models:

| Run | English: alert after the answer (after the tap) | Arabic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1,205 ms (1,710) | 665 ms (1,019) |
| 2 | 560 ms (980) | 1,211 ms (1,553) |
| 3 | 1,572 ms (2,159) | 737 ms (1,027) |

Three more runs of the whole file (with the consent case below) passed too: English 1,594, 609
and 1,053 ms after the answer; Arabic 1,476, 1,025 and 543 ms. The kiosk's taps took 58 to 774
ms each.

**While the cassette was recorded, the budget failed.** Recording uses the live local model: the
Qwen3.5-9B answered the complaint on this 18 GB machine, beside the dev stack, the e2e stack and
Docker. Memory was under pressure (about 8 GB compressed, heavy swapping).
- **Arabic.** It failed twice, at 2,530 ms and 6,431 ms. In the second run, one tap took 4.1 s,
  although taps use no model.
- **English.** It passed once, at 1,751 ms. It failed once in the first recording run; that run's
  message was not kept, because the next run overwrote its results.

These runs are not counted as passes. The runs above, without the live model, all passed. See
docs/FLAKY.md.

## Not changed, and follow-ups

- **The gap for ages 5 to 15 (ADR-035).** Only hard to wake alerts there. A 9-year-old's seizure,
  non-blanching rash or stiff neck is recorded and shown, without an alert. The scenarios pin this
  behaviour. Closing the gap (their red answers, or a paediatric early-warning score) is a clinical
  decision for the owner: SHE-327 (needs-owner).
- **The eval suite measures the extractor alone.** SHE-314's `child-parent-unsure-waking`
  baseline still records its timeout, which is true of the extractor; the product now reads that
  answer without it.
