# M1-12: the intake and nurse API routes, verified

Status: every acceptance criterion met on 27 September 2026 against the real app and the isolated
test stack (mapping at the end). The core routes and runtime were merged earlier (ADR-031, PR #24);
this change adds their verification and fixes what it found. The PR, its CI and the merge commit
are in the Linear SHE-303 done report. Research prototype; synthetic personas only.

## Where it ran

- **Isolated test stack** (`make test-stack` = `python3 scripts/stack.py up --stack careflow-test`):
  Compose project `careflow-test` on Postgres 15432, Valkey 16379 and HAPI FHIR 18080, the
  owner's dev stack (`careflow`, 5432/6379/8080) left running and untouched. The seven synthetic
  personas were seeded into its HAPI (`load_fhir: 7 persona bundles upserted; 7 patients now`).
  All containers of both stacks: 2.62 GiB (budget 6 GiB), measured by `docker stats`.
- API tests use a throwaway `careflow_test` database on that Postgres (Alembic plus the intake
  checkpoint tables, as `make migrate`), a stream of their own on its Valkey, and its HAPI through
  `FHIR_BASE_URL` (one override for every FHIR call, ADR-046).
- Models: none in CI. The flow's free-text answers were recorded once from the local
  Qwen3.5-9B MLX model (`mlx-community/Qwen3.5-9B-MLX-4bit@938d891`) through
  `scripts/with_llm.sh`, which started and then stopped `mlx_lm.server`; every later run replays
  those 3 replies (`services/api/tests/fixtures/flow/providers.cassette.json`).

## What the verification found and fixed

- **A decision without a suggestion answered 500** after it had taken effect (the decision was
  applied and its row written; the response dropped the required, null `suggestion_id`). With no
  triage model installed (the documented fallback) every decision did this. Fixed; the flow test
  asserts the null `suggestion_id`.
- **Failed FHIR write-backs were acknowledged** and never retried, and **a Redis transport error
  ended the event consumer** (ADR-043; the dead-letter part came from the `fix-events-dead-letter`
  worktree). `services/api/tests/test_events_recovery.py`: an outage is retried until the write
  succeeds (4 deferred deliveries, then written once), a rejection is dead-lettered with its
  reason, and the consumer survives failures of `XREADGROUP`, `XGROUP CREATE` and `XAUTOCLAIM`.
- **Two FHIR endpoints**: check-in, write-back and task completion read port 8080 from the
  adapter file while the health check and the briefing read `FHIR_BASE_URL`. One override now.
- **Contract gaps** found by schemathesis, fixed on the side the contract says: 400 for an
  unparseable body, 429 on the five rate-limited routes, 404 on the companion routes, the
  `ws-ticket` session-channel rule, the `Allow` header of a 405, a null `display_name` in
  `/v1/auth/me`, a null body to the dev vitals simulator, and the missing checkpoint tables that
  made the worklist and the doctor's list answer 500 in the test database.
- **The admin status** called a missing urgency model "ok"; it says "degraded" now, and the port
  details say "replay" when providers replay a cassette.

## Known limits and follow-ups

- A task completion while HAPI is down is not retried: the fact stands and the HAPI Task stays
  open until a later completion call. Durable outbound retries belong to SHE-283 [M4-03].
- The flow test needs HAPI, which CI does not run; CI runs the contract tests (Postgres and
  Valkey) and skips the flow with its reason. `make test-integration` runs everything locally.
- The recording pins the extraction prompt: changing the prompt or the templates requires
  recording again with the local model (the test's docstring has the command).

Decisions: ADR-043 (event bus), ADR-046 (verification). Synthetic personas and simulated devices
only; nothing here is clinical validation.
