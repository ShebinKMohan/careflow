## Deviations from the issue text

- **The port returns `EhrFetch`, not `EhrContext`.** `EhrContext` was already the M0-02 contract for the `ehr_context` block. `EhrFetch` wraps it with the status, the patient reference, the demographics (as `StructuredFacts.patient`), the appointments, `fetched_at` and `errors`.
- **Fact keys.** They are the M0-02 keys `ehr_context.allergies`, `ehr_context.conditions` and `ehr_context.medications`, not `ehr_allergy`, `ehr_condition` and `ehr_medication`.
- **Appointments are not facts.** The M0-04 mapping table calls them check-in context, so they come back as `EhrFetch.appointments`.
- **New contract parts.** `AllergyFact` carries the criticality, and the optional `FhirSource.version_id` carries `source_ref.version_id`.
- **Identifier systems.** The adapter has `identifier_systems` (keypad and MR) instead of one `patient_identifier_system`. The personas are found by kiosk keypad id and Synthea patients by MR number; both were checked on the loaded data.
- **The breaker counts answers as successes.** `not_found` and `ambiguous` count as successes, because the EHR answered.
