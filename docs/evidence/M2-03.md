# M2-03: Arabic scenarios and the per-language extraction report (SHE-290)

Status: 28 September 2026. ADR-034 built the Arabic conversation and extraction; this is its test
phase (ADR-055). Research prototype; synthetic data only; not for clinical use. The Arabic was not
validated by native speakers, and its numbers stay in caveated technical reports (`publish: false`).

## What changed

- **Seeded gold** (`eval/src/careflow_eval/sampler.py`): 50 Gulf and 50 Levantine plans (seed 7) in
  the English category mix; the rules engine sets each expected alert on the gold.
- **Drafts from written answers** (`careflow-eval generate --suite intake_ar`): each turn has its
  Arabic text, an English gloss and the gold keys it states. The loader checks numbers and unsure
  answers in the Arabic.
- **The review's back-translation stage** (`eval/src/careflow_eval/backtranslation.py`, `review.py`):
  every answer is back-translated blind, judged against its gloss, and must reach chrF 40 before the
  judge sees the scenario. The rows are recorded in the review log and a receipt.
- **The harness**: `extraction_ar`; `careflow-eval run extraction --lang en|ar|all`;
  `eval/reports/extraction.json` and `.md` (missed alerts first); span counts per case.
- **Intake**: the recorder runs a fixture in its language; 17 Arabic fixtures in
  `services/intake/tests/fixtures/extraction/ar/`.
- **The explicit-answer reader** (`services/intake/src/careflow_intake/tap.py`): leading fillers
  (والله، يعني، امم …; "um", "honestly") are skipped before the unsure check, after the eval found
  "والله ما أدري" read as no (below).

## The corpus and its review

100 scenarios, 50 Gulf and 50 Levantine, all approved (`eval/datasets/intake_ar/`, dataset
`eb39e4e16fee0f8837c1b9568036d76ce95462b65442408c04f0432c30aea0b3`). Expected alerts per rule:
chest pain 14, FAST 10, sepsis NEWS2 10, NG143 red 4, NG143 amber 4, child hard to wake 4.

| Step | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Authoring pre-check (scratch, not the review) | 120 of 476 answers flagged in 72 scenarios (92 misreadings, 28 chrF only); rewritten, then 33 in 25; rewritten again |
| Local review 1 (`local-runs/20260928-064756-795049-review`) | back-translation: 96 of 100 pass; Qwen judge: 45 of 96 approved |
| Codex review 1 (`review-receipts/20260928-072058-725726`) | 48 of 51 approved; 4 rewritten drafts turned back (no local check yet) |
| Local reviews 2 and 3 | back-translation checked 7, then 2 (2 reused); 2 chrF flips rewritten; Qwen approved 3 |
| Codex review 2 (`review-receipts/20260928-075959-348582`) | 4 of 4 approved |
| Total | 100 approved: 48 by `agent:local/qwen3.5-9b-4bit`, 52 by `agent:codex/gpt-6-astra` |

Every decision, with its reasons, back-translation rows and model evidence, is in
`eval/datasets/review-log.jsonl`, `eval/datasets/REVIEW.md` and `eval/datasets/review-receipts/`.
Qwen's judge rejected 57 times, nearly always misreading the rules (see ADR-055); Codex rejected
3 times, fairly, and those answers were rewritten. Codex used 13 CLI calls of the issue's 200,
including one format probe. Each Codex call's model (`gpt-6-astra`, effort high, read-only) is
verified in Codex's own session log.

What the 9B model misread in the pre-check (it also extracts): لأ ("no") often as "Yes"; صرله/صرلها
("it's been") as "diarrhea", "cough", "seizure"; تنين ("two") as "Snake" or "Nine"; وايد ("very")
as "waited"; سن (tooth/age), نفسي (breath/myself), حالي (myself/current); منيح ("fine") as "weak";
دينة (ear) as "religion". The approved set therefore leans toward wording this model reads, and its
scores are optimistic for real speech (ADR-055).

September 2026, Qwen3.5-9B MLX 4-bit (`938d891…`), prompt `extraction_v2_ar`, 20-second timeout.
Recorded to `eval/replays/extraction_ar/`; report `eval/reports/extraction_ar/20260928-081617.json`.

Arabic free-text facts are scored by presence and English ones by exact value: the two columns'
field scores are not comparable. These are technical measurements on synthetic data that no
native speaker validated; never an accuracy percentage.

**Missed expected alerts**, listed first in the report: `child-parent-unsure-waking` (English,
the baseline's recorded timeout) and `ar-gul-child-01` (Gulf, `NG143_RED_FLAG`). In the latter
the parent's "مدري، مو متأكدة" to "is your child hard to wake?" was extracted as `no`. Of the 15
`unknown` gold facts, 10 came back `no` (radiation 4, rigors 3, face droop 2, hard to wake 1). The
intake graph's explicit-answer reader reads 9 of those replies as `unknown`, and an unsure reading
beats the model's no there. The tenth began with "والله"; the reader now skips such fillers.

Also found: `pain_present` (0 of 36) and `symptoms` (0 of 20) are never returned, as in English;
`pain_location` 22 of 36; one breathlessness "yes, a little" (إي، شوي) was not returned.
