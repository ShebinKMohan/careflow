# M2-01: the Arabic right-to-left UI, checked and verified

Status: 27 September 2026, SHE-273. The core was merged earlier (ADR-032). This change adds its
gates and verification, and fixes what they found (ADR-050). Research prototype; synthetic
personas only; not for clinical use. There is no native-speaker review of the Arabic. Every check
below is a machine check, and no Arabic accuracy figure is given anywhere.

## What changed

- **The back-translation gate.** `scripts/i18n/backtranslate.py` writes
  `eval/reports/i18n_backtranslation.json` and `.md`, and its offline `--check` runs in
  `make lint`.
- **`safety.` keys in the staff app.** They cover the alerts, "I feel worse", the medical
  questions, and the triage card's "Suggestion only. You decide." and its warnings.
- **The footer's strings** are in `packages/ui/src/messages/*.json`, so the check covers
  "Not for clinical use" too.
- **Arabic rewrites.** 21 `safety.` strings and 21 others were rewritten; five `safety.` strings
  remain unresolved, show their English original, and are listed in `docs/i18n/unresolved.md`.
  The follow-up is SHE-324.
- **`@rtl` Playwright tests** for the kiosk, staff and companion in English and Arabic, with
  screenshots from the pinned Playwright image (`make rtl`).
- **An Arabic real-stack journey** (`e2e/journeys/arabic.spec.ts`).
- **Fixed:**
  - Latin initials in Arabic sentences are isolated;
  - an Arabic phone no longer re-claims its used QR code on a refresh;
  - the phone learns and shows the end of the visit;
  - a visit finished on the phone reaches the EHR again.

## The back-translation check

- **Model.** Task `translation_check`, model `qwen3.5-9b-4bit`, served by `mlx_lm.server` as
  `mlx-community/Qwen3.5-9B-MLX-4bit@938d8919941c6e7efd3c7150eff7fe9d12afa631` (the M0-06 pin),
  on this machine, offline, at no cost.
- **Method.** The model translates the Arabic back to English without seeing the English, then
  judges whether that means the same as the English original. chrF (sacrebleu 2.6.0) also scores
  the pair. A string is flagged when the model says the meaning differs or chrF < 40.
- **First full run.** 449 strings (87 `safety.`) in 12.5 minutes, of which 142 were flagged
  (19 `safety.`). A first attempt of the run stopped when one batch came back with a single item;
  the script now asks such a batch again in halves, keeps a checkpoint, and prints its progress.
- **Now.** 453 strings (kiosk 114, staff 255, companion 82, footer 2; 91 `safety.`); 113 flagged
  (65 judged different in meaning, 48 by chrF only), none of them `safety.`. The five unresolved
  strings pass only because the English is shown beside them; they are listed.
- **Misreadings found and rewritten.** The model misread these Arabic strings:
  - "حرج" (Critical) as "Embarrassment", and "حَرِج" (with the vowel) as "Embarrassed";
  - "صدر {time}" (Raised {time}) as "Chest {time}";
  - "متابعة" (Continue) as "Follow-up";
  - "توقف الاستقبال مبكراً" (Intake stopped early) as "Check-in stopped early";
  - "عرض مكاني في الدور" (See my place in the queue) as "Show floor plan";
  - "ناقص" (missing) as "Low", and "آخر" (Other) as "Last";
  - "نموذج بحثي" (Research prototype, in the footer) as "Search form";
  - several past-tense verbs as orders, for example "أكّده الممرض" (confirmed by the nurse) as
    "Confirm by nurse".
- **The five-attempt rule.**
  - 21 `safety.` strings were rewritten. 9 passed at the first attempt, 5 at the second, 1 at the
    third and 1 at the fourth.
  - 5 still failed after five attempts: `kiosk:safety.child.me`, `staff:safety.alerts.ecgDone`,
    `staff:safety.alerts.overdue`, `staff:safety.alerts.rules.NG143_AMBER_FLAG` and
    `staff:safety.triage.warnings.intake_stopped_early`. They show the English in brackets beside
    the Arabic, and `docs/i18n/unresolved.md` lists every attempt and its back-translation.
  - Most of the five were judged to mean the same on some attempt and failed only on chrF. The
    model always says "performed" for "done", and "I" or "Myself" for "Me".

## The Arabic journey (real stack)

`e2e/journeys/arabic.spec.ts`, persona `routine` (keypad 100005, synthetic), on the real API,
Postgres, Valkey and HAPI:
1. Arabic is picked on the kiosk's first screen and survives a kiosk refresh; the picker is gone.
2. The patient checks in and answers two questions in Arabic on the kiosk (Arabic questions,
   English-coded facts with the patient's Arabic words as their quotes).
3. The QR hand-over: a fresh phone opens in Arabic. The kiosk forgets the patient and returns to
   its first screen, still in Arabic, with both languages on offer.
4. A phone refresh mid-intake keeps the question and the language. The phone finishes the intake.
5. The waiting room. The nurse (in English) sets the level, and the token shows on the phone,
   left to right. HAPI has the Encounter with KTAS-5.
6. A lost connection while the status fails: the last status stays, marked stale in Arabic, with
   "I feel worse". It recovers on reconnection.
7. The patient is called in (the room reads left to right), then seen. The phone says the visit
   has ended, in Arabic, also after a refresh.

The Arabic extraction replies were recorded from the local model into the cassette
(`E2E_RECORD=add`: 4 entries added, none changed). The journeys replay them.

All eight journeys ran on the stack `careflow-e2e` (+30000) from a worktree of this branch, with
the models replayed. The first run after starting the stack's apps is listed as the warm-up.

- **Budgets.** Both are asserted on this machine (and only reported on CI). The warm-up failed the
  "I feel worse" budget. An earlier cold run the same day failed the chest-pain budget at
  2,153 ms; its next three consecutive runs passed (1,208, 1,198 and 1,726 ms). Nothing was retried
  or loosened.
- **Why the times rise.** The nurse's worklist reads every open visit one by one, and the test
  stack held 161 open visits from the day's runs. That is SHE-325 (docs/FLAKY.md).
- **Recording.** While the Arabic replies were being recorded against the local model, the Arabic
  journey passed end to end in 1.1 min.

## Bugs found and fixed

- **A visit finished on the phone never reached the EHR.**
  - Such a patient skips the kiosk's vitals, but the rules keep a partial NEWS2 with every input
    missing. The write-back built a NEWS2 derived from nothing, with no provenance, and safety
    rule 4 refused the whole Bundle.
  - The nine `triage.confirmed` events of the M2-07 journey runs were dead-lettered.
  - Fixed in the builder; `test_a_news2_with_nothing_measured_is_left_out_and_the_rest_is_written`
    fails before and passes after.
- **A refreshed Arabic phone claimed a used QR code.** The language switch after the claim used
  `router.refresh()`, which put the one-time code back in the address bar. A refresh then showed
  "this code didn't work". It is now one full load. The companion `@rtl` test reloads after the
  claim, with a claim stub that refuses a second claim.
- **The phone never learned that the visit had ended.** It kept "go to room ..." after the nurse
  marked the patient seen. The API now tells that token's own phone, and the phone shows the end
  of the visit.
  - `test_a_seen_or_no_show_patients_phone_learns_its_final_state` fails before and passes after.
  - `waiting-room.test.tsx` has two new tests.
- **Latin initials broke inside Arabic sentences.** "M. S." showed as ".M. S". They are isolated
  now; the screenshots show it.
- **Five Arabic safety strings had a wrong or ambiguous meaning.** They were rewritten; see above.
