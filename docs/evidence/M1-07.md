## Recordings and the extraction prompt

- **The recording session.** `scripts/with_llm.sh` recorded the M1-06 live cases and the three scripts with the local model. The new keys changed the extraction prompt, so every cassette was recorded again.
- **v1 misses.** With `extraction_v1`, the live fever case filed "a high fever and chills" under `symptoms`, and the ankle's allergy answer was not extracted.
- **`extraction_v2`** adds two things:
  - use the specific key, not `symptoms`;
  - answers to free-text questions are that key.

  All 3 M1-06 live cases meet their expectations again, for example `infection.fever_reported = yes`, and so do the 3 scripts.
- **One remaining miss.** The model gave `false` for the allergy answer. The extractor now keeps the patient's verified words for a free-text key (fixture `text_answer_as_yes_no`).
- **Pruning.** The script cassettes were pruned to the replies a replay uses.

## Deviations from the issue text

- **Contract additions.** `symptom_onset_at`, `severity`, `pain_location` and `allergies_confirm` are new StructuredFacts facts, so the question keys can match the fact keys. `allergies_confirm` is free text: a yes/no would mean opposite things for its two questions. 16 reviewed oasdiff enum additions come with them.
- **Nurse-coming delivery.** The message comes as the question's `notice`, or on its own to acknowledge when no screen is left. It is shown once per session.
- **The filter's "you have" pattern needs a hedge word**, so questions like "Do you have chest pain?" are allowed. Drug names are not blocked, because allergy lists contain them; dosing and advice patterns cover the risk.
- **Phrasing acceptance.** A rephrasing must also be exactly one question ending in "?".
- **Checklist skips.** Onset is skipped when a chest-pain or last-known-well time is known. Severity is skipped when a pain score is known. Pain location is skipped for chest pain or no pain.
- **The extraction prompt moved to `extraction_v2`.** Free-text keys keep the verified quote when the model gives a yes/no. The M1-06 live cassettes were recorded again.
- **Urgency stays a stub in `build_real_ports`.** The M1-04 model needs its local artifact; M1-12 wires it.
