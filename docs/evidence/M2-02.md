# M2-02: Arabic speech-to-text and text-to-speech, verified (SHE-278)

Status: 28 September 2026. ADR-033 (the engines) and ADR-058 (this test phase). Research prototype; synthetic data only; not for clinical use.

## What changed

- **Tests of the engines, with fixtures (CI).**
  - `test_cohere_transcribe_stt.py`: the gated model is never downloaded; 401 and 403 are the owner's step; the model card's Arabic inference; MPS, else CPU.
  - `test_mms_tts.py`: sentence units at ؟ and ؛; resampling; barge-in; warm-up once; a missing model or extra.
  - `test_kiosk_speech.py`: the per-language factory and turn settings.
  - `test_kiosk_ws.py`: four socket cases: an Arabic session, a lost connection, engines that fail to start and then recover, and an engine that fails mid-session.
  - `apps/kiosk/tests/ws-voice-client.test.ts`: two client cases, a denied microphone and `speech_unavailable`.
  - `test_mlx_whisper_stt.py`: the energy floor.
- **Checks on the real models (`@pytest.mark.models`, this Mac only).**
  - `test_speech_models.py`: the Arabic MMS → Whisper round trip, and an Arabic kiosk session on the real engines.
  - `test_live_voice.py` (English) now carries the marker too.
- **The end of a spoken turn per language.** `config/providers.yaml` `speech.turn` (ADR-058). English ends after 400 ms of silence, Arabic after 700 ms; Smart Turn no longer ends turns early.
- **No turn from near-silence.** A frame under -60 dBFS is never speech.
- **An engine that stops mid-session** now tells the kiosk: `speech_unavailable`, then a 1011 close.
- **`CohereTranscribeSTT`** is its own class.
- **The speech eval.**
  - `eval/speech/make_clips.py`, `run_wer.py`, `run_turns.py` and `run_latency.py`, over `eval/datasets/speech/`;
  - the shared pieces are in `careflow_eval.speech`, tested in `eval/tests/test_speech.py`;
  - `jiwer` 4.0.0 is locked.

## Product defects the checks found, fixed

1. **Answers were cut in two at the first pause, in both languages.**
   - **Found by.** The first real-model check. A TTS-voiced "عندي ألم في صدري من ساعة." reached the kiosk as "عدي".
   - **Cause.** Smart Turn v3 at the 0.5 threshold judged almost every prefix complete: Arabic 0.89 to 0.99, unfinished English 0.63 to 0.72. Every turn therefore ended after 200 ms of silence.
   - **Scale.** Through the service's own turn logic, 30 of 261 English and 41 of 506 Arabic answers split into two turns.
   - **Fix.** Per-language silence ends: 0 of 261 English and 3 of 506 Arabic answers split.
2. **A turn of near-silence could become an answer the patient never gave.**
   - **Whisper.** mlx-whisper writes "Thank you." (English) and "ترجمة نانسي قنقر" (Arabic) into digital silence and faint noise, with `no_speech_prob` 0.0.
   - **The trigger.** On one clip, the Silero VAD, reset after the turn, opened a second turn on MMS's -70 dBFS tail.
   - **Fix.** The -60 dBFS floor. No phantom turn remains in any setting.
3. **A speech engine stopping mid-session left the patient talking to nothing.** The kiosk now gets `speech_unavailable` and a 1011 close, and falls back to typing, as for a failed start.

## The speech eval, 28 September 2026, on this Mac (Apple M3 Pro, 18 GB)

**Clips.** 767 answers over 530 distinct clips; 71 MB of WAVs, gitignored. Re-voicing reproduces them byte for byte.

| | Sentences | Scenario turns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English (Kokoro) | 15 | 246 |
| Gulf Arabic (MMS-TTS) | 15 | 238 |
| Levantine Arabic (MMS-TTS) | 15 | 238 |

**Speech-to-text** (`run_wer.py`). mlx-whisper `mlx-community/whisper-large-v3-turbo@a4aaeec0636e6fef84abdcbe3544cb2bf7e9f6fb`, each clip whole, 0.67 to 0.76 s a clip over two runs:

| Language | Set | n | WER | CER |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| English | sentences | 15 | 0.017 | 0.014 |
| English | scenario turns | 246 | 0.040 | 0.034 |
| Arabic, Gulf | sentences | 15 | 0.529 | 0.193 |
| Arabic, Levantine | sentences | 15 | 0.528 | 0.179 |
| Arabic, Gulf | scenario turns | 238 | 0.514 | 0.208 |
| Arabic, Levantine | scenario turns | 238 | 0.546 | 0.224 |

- **English.** 1 of 15 sentences and 19 of 246 scenario turns differ at all. All but one differ only in a number word written as digits ("seven out of ten" heard as "7 out of 10"), which the issue's normalisation counts as an error. The one other is the answer "3" heard as "3A".
- **Arabic.** The Arabic rows measure the MMS voice and Whisper together. MMS reads dialect words poorly: "عندي" comes back as "عدي", "زين" as "زي". They are machine measurements on synthetic audio, not an Arabic accuracy figure; `publish: false`.
- **Cohere Transcribe Arabic.** `not_run` (gated).
- **Run-to-run variation.** Two runs gave the same WER everywhere. The Arabic scenario CER moved in the fourth decimal (0.2160, then 0.2157): Whisper's temperature fallback samples when a decode looks unreliable. The table is the second run, the committed report.

**Where a turn ends** (`run_turns.py`): the ADR-058 table. The configured settings split no English answer and 3 of 506 Arabic answers, and leave no phantom turn.

**Latency** (`run_latency.py`). The real engines in-process, 20 answers per language in real time over `/kiosk/audio`, stage times from the voice service's own clock:

| Stage (p50 / p95, ms) | English | Arabic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| End of speech → transcript (the silence wait, then Whisper) | 1,155 / 1,203 | 1,560 / 1,675 |
| Transcript → API response (stubbed) | 0.6 / 0.8 | 0.6 / 1.4 |
| API response → first TTS byte (Kokoro / MMS) | 333 / 386 | 261 / 297 |
| **Total** | **1,492 / 1,563** | **1,808 / 1,973** |

No answer split in these sessions. **The extraction is not included:** with the local model it adds its own time before the API answers (M2-12: 6 to 8 s for a typed answer). M6-01 measures the whole turn.

## The handoff's checks in the actual voice service

| Check | Where it is proved |
| --- | --- |
| Language selection | The kiosk's `start` carries `language`. For `ar`, the service builds the Arabic engines and posts turns with `language: ar` (`test_an_arabic_session_builds_arabic_engines_and_posts_arabic_turns`); on the real engines, `test_an_arabic_kiosk_session_on_the_real_engines_speaks_and_writes_nothing`. Whisper gets the session language, never detected (`test_the_session_language_reaches_whisper_never_detected`). |
| Microphone denial | `ws-voice-client.test.ts`: a denied microphone rejects `start()` and opens no socket; the intake then shows "voice failed" and keeps typing and taps. In the browser, `consent-voice` (M2-05) shows no `getUserMedia` before consent. |
| Interruption | `test_speech_during_the_question_barges_in_and_becomes_the_next_turn`; MMS stops rendering the remaining sentences on a barge-in, including one queued behind another session (`test_mms_a_barge_in_…`). |
| Disconnect and reconnect | `test_a_lost_connection_can_start_again_for_the_same_session` |
| Error recovery | Engines that fail to start: `speech_unavailable`, 1011, and the next start works. An engine that fails mid-session: `speech_unavailable`, 1011 (new). API failures are error messages and the session stays open. |
| No raw audio left | No file opened for writing during a session, with the fixture engines and with the real Arabic engines (above). |
| The Arabic voice's licence | MMS-TTS is CC-BY-NC-4.0, non-commercial only. It must be replaced before any commercial release (ADR-058; `eval/datasets/speech/README.md`; the report's caveats). |
| One actual speech journey | M2-12 `consent-voice` (a): the real voice service transcribes a synthetic recording with its local Whisper (SHE-311). |
